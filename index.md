# Seam Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how the Seam Studio Android app handles your information.

## Images and project content

Seam Studio accesses images you select through Android’s file picker to display, edit, save, and export your designs.

Project content—including imported image copies, text, shapes, layout information, and recovery drafts—is stored in the app’s private storage on your device. The app also stores preferences, such as whether you have dismissed the introductory guide.

Seam Studio does not upload your designs to servers operated by the developer.

## Accounts, advertising, and analytics

Seam Studio does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics tracking, or an integrated crash-reporting service.

## Exported files

When you export a design, Seam Studio writes PNG files to the folder you choose through Android’s folder picker.

If you choose a cloud-storage provider, that provider may upload or synchronize the exported files according to its own settings and privacy policy.

## Android backup and device transfer

Seam Studio allows Android’s backup and device-transfer features. Eligible app data, which may include projects, imported image copies, recovery drafts, and preferences, may be backed up or transferred depending on your device, Android settings, backup limits, and backup provider.

These services are managed by Android and your device or backup provider. Backup is not guaranteed.

## Storage and security

Project files are stored using Android’s app-private storage protections. Exported files are stored in your selected destination and are subject to that destination’s access controls.

No storage or backup method can guarantee complete security or protection against data loss.

## Retention and deletion

Saved projects remain in app storage until you delete them or remove the app’s data. You can delete saved projects from the project list.

Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app removes its local private data. Previously created backups may remain under your backup provider’s retention rules and may be restored later.

Deleting a project does not delete your original source images or exported PNG files. Delete those separately using your file manager or storage provider.

The developer cannot remotely access, delete, or recover projects stored on your device.

## Support communications

If you email seamstudioapp@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your inquiry and troubleshoot reported problems.

Support emails are handled through Gmail, subject to Google’s privacy practices. We retain correspondence while needed to resolve your inquiry and maintain relevant support records. You may request deletion by contacting the same address, subject to any applicable legal retention requirements.

Please avoid sending sensitive images or information unless necessary for your support request.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The updated policy will show a revised date.
